## Firm Background

Romero Law Group is a boutique employment law firm that has been protecting the rights of workers for more than 20 years. Founded by attorney Peter A. Romero, the firm practices employment law exclusively — it is not a general practice that takes employment cases on the side. Every attorney at the firm focuses on the laws that govern the workplace.

The firm represents employees across Long Island, New York City, Westchester, and Northern New Jersey who have been underpaid, harassed, discriminated against, retaliated against, or wrongfully pushed out of their jobs. From restaurant workers and delivery drivers owed unpaid wages to professionals facing discrimination or negotiating severance, Romero Law Group takes on employers of every size — "We stand tall against Goliath."

Because the firm also advises employers on handbooks, policies, and dispute resolution, its attorneys understand how companies and their lawyers build a defense, and they use that insight to anticipate the other side's strategy on behalf of workers.

Romero Law Group is admitted to practice in New York and New Jersey state courts, as well as the U.S. District Courts for the Southern and Eastern Districts of New York, the District of New Jersey, and the U.S. Courts of Appeals for the Second and Third Circuits. The firm serves Spanish-speaking clients directly through its bilingual staff.

At Romero Law Group, "You're not a case number here." Clients get direct access to experienced employment lawyers who return calls, answer questions, and explain every stage of the process.

## Core Service Offerings

### Wage & Hour Claims

Romero Law Group's foundation is recovering wages that employers owe. Under New York law, workers may be able to recover up to six years of unpaid wages, plus liquidated damages, statutory damages, and attorneys' fees. The firm handles individual cases as well as class and collective actions.

### Wage & Hour Cases We Handle

-   Unpaid Overtime
-   Unpaid Minimum Wage
-   Off-the-Clock Work
-   Employee Misclassification (Independent Contractor / Exempt Status)
-   Late Payment of Wages
-   Restaurant & Tipped Worker Claims (Tip Theft, Illegal Tip Pools, Improper Tip Credit)
-   Spread-of-Hours Pay

## Best Practices & Recommendations for Workers

-   Keep Your Own Record of Hours Worked, Including Breaks and Off-the-Clock Tasks
-   Save Pay Stubs, Wage Notices, and Tip Records
-   Document Harassment or Discrimination in Writing (Dates, Witnesses, What Was Said)
-   Report Problems Through Your Employer's Complaint Process and Keep Copies
-   Do Not Sign a Severance Agreement Without Having It Reviewed
-   Do Not Delete Texts, Emails, or Messages That Relate to Your Claim
-   Be Aware of Deadlines — Claims Have Strict Time Limits
-   Contact an Experienced Employment Lawyer Early

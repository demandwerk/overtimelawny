**Last updated: November 5, 2026**

Romero Law Group, PLLC ("we," "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## Information you give us

When you contact us through our consultation form, by phone, by chat or by email, you may give us your name, phone number, email address and information about your situation. You choose what to share. Please do not send sensitive documents until we have agreed to represent you.

Contacting us does not create an attorney-client relationship. Information you send before we agree to represent you may not be treated as privileged, so avoid sending highly sensitive details through the website.

## Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we use cookies and similar technologies to understand how people find and use the site. This may include your IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, and how you reached us.

We use the following third-party services, which may collect this information under their own privacy policies:

- **Google Analytics (GA4)** to measure website traffic and usage.
- **Google Ads** to measure the effectiveness of our advertising and, where enabled, to show our ads to previous visitors.
- **Ngage** to provide our live chat service.
- **UserWay** to provide accessibility tools.

You can control cookies through your browser settings and opt out of personalized Google ads in your Google ad settings.

## How we use your information

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, evaluate whether we can help you, schedule consultations, communicate with you, operate and improve the website, and measure our marketing. We may also use it to comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

## How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the website, communicate with you and measure our marketing, or when required by law, court order or to protect our rights.

## Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no website or email transmission is completely secure. We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described here and to meet our legal and professional obligations.

## Your choices

You can contact us to ask what information we hold about you, to request a correction or deletion, or to ask us to stop contacting you. New York and New Jersey residents may have additional rights under applicable law.

## Children

This website is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.

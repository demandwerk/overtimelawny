### Manual Workers Must Be Paid Weekly

If you spend more than 25% of your working time doing physical labor, New York law generally requires your employer to pay you every week, not every other week. Workers who were paid late may be owed significant compensation. [Read more about weekly pay for manual workers](/ny-law-requiring-weekly-pay-for-manual-workers/).

This often applies to:

- Construction workers and laborers
- Custodial, porter and maintenance workers
- Factory workers and machine operators
- Warehouse pickers and packers
- Restaurant workers

We have represented employees at workplaces such as the Middletown Industrial Park in Orange County. [Find out if you're owed wages](/contact-an-unpaid-wages-lawyer/).

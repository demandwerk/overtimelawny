### New York City Gives Workers Some of the Strongest Protections in the Country

Workers in the five boroughs are protected by federal and New York State law, and also by the [New York City Human Rights Law (NYCHRL)](/new-york-city-human-rights-law-for-queens-brooklyn-the-bronx-and-harlem-upper-manhattan/). The NYCHRL covers more protected classes than federal or state law, including:

- Age, race, color, religion and national origin
- Gender, gender identity and sexual orientation, including sexual harassment
- Disability, pregnancy and lactation accommodations
- Immigration or citizenship status
- Caregiver status, credit history, salary history and unemployment status
- Arrest or conviction record
- Status as a victim of domestic violence, stalking or sex offenses

Our lawyers know the NYCHRL along with state and federal law, and we use whichever gives you the strongest claim.

### Unpaid Wages and Overtime in NYC

New York's wage and hour laws give workers more protection than federal law. Under the New York Labor Law you may be able to:

- Recover unpaid wages going back six years, plus interest
- Recover an additional equal amount as liquidated ("double") damages
- Claim an extra hour of "spread of hours" pay if you earn minimum wage and your workday runs longer than 10 hours
- Recover tips or service charges your employer kept
- Bring a claim regardless of your immigration status

We regularly represent [restaurant and tipped workers](/restaurant-workers-and-tipped-employees/), and New York City [fast food workers](/rights-for-new-york-city-fast-food-workers-changes/) have additional scheduling rights. See our page on [overtime pay and unpaid wages](/overtime-pay-and-unpaid-wages/) for more.
